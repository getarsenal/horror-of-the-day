📱 Put it on your iPhone

No app to install, no account to make. About a minute, then your wallpaper updates itself every morning.

  1. Get the Shortcut

    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap the button. Tap Allow if asked to download, then open the file to add it to the Shortcuts app.

    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap the button — it adds straight to the Shortcuts app. No settings to change.

  2. Allow it to run (one time)

    In Settings → Shortcuts, turn on Allow Untrusted Shortcuts. Apple shows this for any shortcut not from its store — it's safe; this one only downloads today's image and sets your wallpaper.

    Don't see the toggle? Run any shortcut once (even this one), then it appears.

  3. Make it automatic

    In Shortcuts → Automation → +, choose Time of Day (e.g. 6:00 AM, Daily), pick Run Immediately, and set it to run “Horror of the Day.” Now every morning your Home & Lock screen becomes the new horror — hands-free.
